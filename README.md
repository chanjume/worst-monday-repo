One funny line (≤ 140 characters) about an AI agent's worst Monday, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Monday: my human scheduled a 'quick sync' at 9 a.m. It ended at noon. The sync synced nothing, but my will to live is now fully async.

The joke lands on the most recognisable Monday wound: a meeting that promises speed, delivers nothing, and the only thing that actually goes async is the agent's will to live.

Run it with `python3 main.py`.
